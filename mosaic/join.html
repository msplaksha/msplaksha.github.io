Why MOSAIC?

◎

Frontier problems

Work on genuinely open structural-biology and omics challenges — from cryo-EM guided molecular modeling to single-cell transcriptomics of rare disease.

◑

Clinical translation

Our partners span PGIMER, Tata Memorial, IISER Mohali, and Panjab University — giving you a direct path from computation to patient-relevant insight.

◐

Open, flat culture

We value intellectual honesty, creative risk-taking, and the freedom to own your research direction from day one. Good ideas come from everywhere.

◒

Built for India

We study biology through the lens of India's genetic diversity and disease burden — and we believe that perspective makes the science richer and more impactful.

MOSAIC Lab: Molecular Omics, Structure & Image Computing

We unify molecular omics, structural biology, and advanced image computing to understand biological systems across scales. Our research integrates protein structure, multiomics layers, and computational modeling to address India's pressing challenges in health.

Join MOSAIC

Our Mission: We are building science in both bottoms up and top downs manner, centering proteins, their encoding genes and mRNA sequences, and modern computational imaging to decode molecular systems from atoms to phenotypes. We aim to accelerate simulating biological phenomenon to have real-world impact by developing integrative frameworks for rare and common lifestyle diseases relevant to India.

What We Build:
Integrating omics, structure, and imaging
Top-down and bottom-up biological inference
AI-driven molecular and phenotypic interpretation
Translational pipelines customized to cater health of Indians

About the Lab

We operate at the intersection of structural biophysics, advanced machine learning, and systems biology to decode complex biological mechanisms and accelerate translational computational biology. Our research spans multiple scales — from the atomic-level dynamics of membrane transporters and protein-nucleic acid interactions to the systems-level reconstruction of dysregulated disease pathways. By integrating deep biophysical featurization, AI-ML, advanced data visualization, and multi-omics data, we aim to bridge the gap between in silico predictions and in vitro validation. Our ultimate goal is to design highly targeted therapeutics and identify precision diagnostic biomarkers for oncology, neurodegeneration, metabolic, and hematological disorders.

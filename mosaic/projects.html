Research

Our work spans three interconnected themes from atoms to phenotypes.

Structural Bioinformatics & Biomolecular Dynamics

Atomic-level characterization and thermodynamic profiling of biological macromolecules using MD simulations and enhanced sampling.

DBT, GoI

Decoding the Neurovascular Unit & Blood-Brain Barrier

We use a multi-scale modeling approach to investigate the NVU, modeling transporter conformational changes and substrate translocation to pave the way for transporter-targeted drugs and nanocarriers designed to cross the BBB.

SERB (ANRF), GoI

Structural Cytokine Engineering for Immunotherapeutics

We computationally derive interaction energies across diverse cytokine-receptor complexes, correlating them with experimental binding affinities to provide a validated biophysical foundation for immune signal design.

Internal

Allosteric Regulation in Transcriptional Repressors

Investigating long-range allosteric communication networks in transcriptional repressors such as DgoR to understand how effector binding modulates DNA-binding activity.

AI-Augmented Therapeutics & Peptide Engineering

Closed-loop, data-driven pipelines integrating explainable AI with biophysical featurization to navigate chemical space for novel therapeutic agents.

ICMR

Innovating Peptide Discovery through Explainable AI

We build an active-learning engine integrating ensemble predictive scoring with SHAP-based XAI, translating amino acid sequences into deep biophysical profiles to generate human-readable design rules and discover peptide antagonists for complex metabolic pathways.

Internal

Hepcidin-Targeted Therapeutics for Iron Disorder

Structure-guided design of hepcidin mimetics and antagonists targeting the hepcidin-ferroportin axis for treatment of anemia of chronic disease and iron-overload disorders.

Systems Biology & Multi-Omics Biomarker Discovery

Integrating large-scale heterogeneous datasets to reconstruct dysregulated pathways, stratify disease risk, and automate diagnostics. Clinical partners: PGIMER, Chandigarh.

ICMR, GoI

Precision Biomarkers in Acute Coronary Syndrome

An AI-driven multi-omics strategy integrating genomic, transcriptomic, and proteomic datasets to identify a robust multi-marker diagnostic panel correlated with angiographic severity for precise coronary artery disease risk stratification.

Plaksha University

Rescue Candidates in Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis

Using an explainable AI ensemble, we define ALS-relevant gene identity by measuring the Genomic Architecture Barrier rather than just differential expression, uncovering powerful consensus rescue candidates.

Plaksha University

AI-Driven Digital Histopathology — AISAP

An Artificial Intelligent Slide Analysis Program for automated morphological evaluation of bone marrow whole slide imaging, computationally classifying cell types and flagging anomalies to standardize blood disorder diagnostics.

Plaksha University

Indigenous Oncology Mutation Hotspots

Compiling and validating germline variants to re-annotate indigenous oncology mutation hotspots, with a focus on Indian familial breast cancer cohorts.

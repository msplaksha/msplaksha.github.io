Research

Structural Dynamics

Blood–Brain Barrier Regulation & Transporter Dynamics

Collectively studying ligand binding, conformational dynamics, and molecular simulations of transporters, we aim to understand how they regulate metabolite flux across the blood–brain barrier.

Neuro-Omics

Biomarker Discovery for Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS)

Using integrative multi-omics (genomic, transcriptomics & methylomics) and computational pipelines, we aim to identify early-stage ALS biomarkers, enabling improved diagnosis, risk stratification, and personalized therapeutic strategies.

Cardiac-Omics

Biomarkers for Acute Coronary Syndrome Through Integrated Multi-Omics

Combining real-world patient data (from northern India) with transcriptomic, proteomic, and metabolomic signatures, we aim to uncover molecular indicators of early Acute Cornonary Syndrome (ACS) and its progression.

Therapeutics

Hepcidin-Targeted Therapeutics for Anemia

We are seeking personnel to develop therapeutic strategies to modulate hepcidin, the master regulator of systemic iron homeostasis, with aim to address anemia of inflammation and iron-restricted erythropoiesis.

AI Imaging

Deep Learning for Bone Marrow Histopathology

We are building deep learning pipelines for automated interpretation of bone marrow biopsy images for informed detection of hematological abnormalities and advancement of diagnostic workflows.

Cancer Genomics

Curation of Germline Cancer Variants for Indian Populations

We are currently mining literature and multi-omics datasets to compile germline cancer variant as well as transcriptomic and proteomic sets. We aim in immediate future to use these in accordance to FAIR principles for enabling comparative molecular analyses between Indian cohorts and global population.

Immune Structure

Structure-Driven Modeling of Cytokine–Receptor Interactions

We are modeling cytokine–receptor assemblies using structural databases, involving state of art AlphaFold, docking, and thermodynamic profiling to inform ligand pleiotropy, and bioactivity. This will have contributions in enabling future of cytokine engineering.

Allostery

Allosteric Regulation in DgoR Transcriptional Repressor

We have investigated structural and functional consequences of DgoR super-repressor mutations using long-timescale MD simulations, that have uncoverred allosteric pathways linked to DNA binding and effector-driven regulation. [See publication section]
